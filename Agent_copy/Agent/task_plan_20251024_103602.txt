*(Objective: To efficiently secure all essential travel components and documentation in parallel.)*

*   **2.1 [IMMEDIATE] Secure Visas & Documents:**
    *   Based on the finalized destination, research visa requirements on the official embassy website.

*   **2.2 [PARALLEL] Book Major Transit & Lodging:**
    *   **Transportation:** Use flight aggregators (Google Flights, Skyscanner) to find the best options. **Set price alerts** for your route to automate monitoring. Book main flights/trains.
    *   **Accommodation:** Simultaneously, research and book lodging (hotels, Airbnb). Use map views to ensure locations are convenient for your planned activities. Read reviews from the last 6 months.

*   **2.3 [ONGOING] Purchase Insurance & Organize Confirmations:**
    *   **Purchase Insurance:** After your first major non-refundable booking (flight or hotel), purchase a comprehensive travel insurance policy.
    *   **Centralize Documents:** As soon as a booking is made, save the confirmation PDF and email to a dedicated, shared cloud folder (e.g., Google Drive, Dropbox) accessible to all travelers. Name files clearly (e.g., "Flight Confirmation - John Doe - LAX to LHR.pdf"). This eliminates the need for a massive document hunt later.

*   **Automation & Efficiency Suggestions:**
    *   **Confirmation Management:** Use an app like **TripIt**, which can automatically scan your email inbox for booking confirmations and build a master itinerary for you.
    *   **Price Alerts:** Let technology do the work. Don't check flight prices daily; let the automated alerts tell you when to buy.

*(Objective: To build a smart, enjoyable itinerary by grouping activities and pre-booking essentials.)*

*   **3.1 Research & Map Activities:**
    *   Create a master list of potential sights, tours, restaurants, and experiences in a shared document or map.
    *   As you research, save every point of interest to a custom Google My Maps list. This allows you to visually see where things are located.

*   **3.2 Build a Geographic-Based Itinerary:**
    *   Using your custom map, group activities by neighborhood or area for each day. This drastically reduces travel time and cost between attractions.
    *   Structure a flexible daily schedule, balancing pre-planned activities with free time.

*   **3.3 [PARALLEL] Book Ancillaries:**
    *   **High-Demand Activities:** Book tickets for popular museums, tours, or events that are likely to sell out.
    *   **Key Reservations:** Make reservations for any must-try restaurants.
    *   **Local Transit:** Research the local public transit system. If a multi-day pass is cost-effective, plan to purchase it upon arrival.
    *   **Continue organizing all new confirmations** into your central cloud folder as they are booked.

*   **Automation & Efficiency Suggestions:**
    *   **Collaborative Mapping:** Use **Google My Maps** or **Wanderlog** so all travelers can add points of interest to a shared map, making itinerary planning a visual, collaborative process.
    *   **Offline Maps:** Download the relevant city map in your mapping app for offline access, ensuring you have navigation without needing data.

*(Objective: To execute a final checklist of parallel tasks for a seamless departure.)*

*   **4.1 [PARALLEL] Financial & Home Prep:**

    *   **Review & Backup Documents:** Review your central cloud folder. Download offline copies of all documents to your phone. Print physical copies of critical items (passport, visa, first-night hotel) as a backup.
