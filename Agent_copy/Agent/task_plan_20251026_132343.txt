This plan is presented as a single, continuous workflow.

**Part 1: Preparation (Est. Time: 3-5 minutes)**

*   **Action 1: Review and Organize Workspace.**
    *   Quickly read the recipe to confirm steps.
    *   Gather all ingredients (eggs, milk, butter, salt, pepper) and equipment (non-stick pan, bowl, whisk, spatula, plate) and place them near the stovetop. Ensure the area is clean and clear.

**Part 2: Cooking & Plating (Est. Time: 4-6 minutes)**

*   **Action 2: Initiate Heating & Prepare Egg Mixture (Parallel Tasks).**
    *   Place the non-stick pan on the stove over **medium-low heat** and add the butter or oil.
    *   *While the butter is melting*, crack the eggs into the mixing bowl. Add milk/cream, salt, and pepper.
    *   Whisk the mixture vigorously until uniform and frothy (about 30-45 seconds). By now, the butter should be melted and shimmering, and the pan perfectly heated.

*   **Action 3: Cook the Eggs.**
    *   Pour the egg mixture into the heated pan. Let it set for 15-20 seconds.
    *   Using the spatula, gently push the cooked portions from the edges toward the center, tilting the pan to let the liquid egg fill the gaps.
    *   Continue this slow, continuous folding motion until the eggs are mostly set but still look moist and glossy.

*   **Action 4: Finish and Plate.**
    *   **Immediately** remove the pan from the heat source. The residual heat will complete the cooking.
    *   Instantly transfer the eggs to the serving plate to prevent overcooking.

**Part 3: Shutdown & Cleanup (Est. Time: 5-8 minutes)**

*   **Action 5: Safety Shutdown.**
    *   Turn the stovetop burner completely **OFF**. As a best practice, verbally confirm, "Stove is off."

*   **Action 6: Efficient Cleanup (Parallel Tasks).**
    *   While the pan cools on the stove or a heat-proof surface, wash the mixing bowl, whisk/fork, and any other used utensils. Wipe down the countertop.
    *   Once the pan is cool enough to handle safely, wash it and the spatula.
