**Phase 1: Foundation & Scoping (Complete within Week 1)**
*(Goal: To rapidly finalize core trip parameters by performing research and coordination simultaneously.)*

*   **Action 1.1: Establish Core Parameters (Group Coordination)**
    *   **1.1.1:** Finalize the list of travelers.
    *   **1.1.2:** **(Automation)** Use a scheduling tool like **Doodle Poll** or **When2meet** to quickly find and confirm travel dates that work for everyone.
    *   **1.1.3:** Agree on an all-inclusive budget. **(Automation)** Use a shared **Google Sheet** to document and track the budget in real-time.

*   **Action 1.2: Research & Decide on a Destination (Parallel Task)**
    *   **1.2.1:** While coordinating dates, brainstorm 2-3 potential destinations.
    *   **1.2.2:** **(Parallel & Automated)** For each destination, conduct simultaneous preliminary research on:
        *   **Transportation Costs:** Use **Google Flights/Skyscanner** or **Omio** (for trains/buses) to get quick cost estimates.
        *   **Accommodation Costs:** Use **Google Hotels/Booking.com/Airbnb** to check average prices for your dates.
        *   **Key Attractions:** A quick search to ensure it aligns with the group's interests.
    *   **1.2.3:** Compare the combined estimated costs against the budget and make a final decision.

**Phase 2: Logistics & Booking (Complete within Week 2)**
*(Goal: To efficiently secure all reservations using integrated tools and automated organization.)*

*   **Action 2.1: Book Transportation & Accommodation (Parallel Research)**
    *   **2.1.1:** **(Automation)** Use travel aggregator sites like **Kayak** or **Google Travel** to search for flight/train and hotel bundles or view them side-by-side. This provides a clear picture of the total cost before committing. Set price alerts if you have time to wait for a better deal.
    *   **2.1.2:** Review options, focusing on recent reviews, location convenience (using the map view), and total cost.
    *   **2.1.3:** Book transportation and accommodation. It is often best to book the less flexible item (like flights) first.

*   **Action 2.2: Automate Confirmation Management**
    *   **2.2.1:** **(Automation)** Set up a rule in your email (e.g., a "Trip" label in Gmail) to automatically tag and file all booking confirmation emails.
    *   **2.2.2:** **(Automation)** For a comprehensive master itinerary, forward all confirmation emails to a service like **TripIt** or **Wanderlog**. These apps automatically parse the details and build a timeline, centralizing all booking numbers, addresses, and times.

**Phase 3: Itinerary & Final Preparations (Complete Weeks 3 & 4)**
*(Goal: To finalize the trip details and personal preparations efficiently through parallel tracks.)*

*   **Track A: Itinerary Planning (Parallel)**
    *   **3.1.1:** List key activities, sights, and restaurants.
    *   **3.1.2:** **(Automation)** Use **Google Maps** to "star" or save all locations to a custom list. This helps visualize distances and group activities by neighborhood for a more logical schedule.
    *   **3.1.3:** Check opening hours and book any necessary reservations (tours, restaurants) directly from your research. Add these confirmations to your automated itinerary (Action 2.2.2).

*   **Track B: Personal & Home Prep (Parallel)**
    *   **3.2.1:** Arrange for any necessary pet sitters, house sitters, or mail holds. Notify emergency contacts of your travel dates and share the itinerary.
    *   **3.2.2:** **(Automation)** Use an app like **PackPoint**, which generates a detailed packing list based on your destination, trip duration, weather forecast, and planned activities from your itinerary.
    *   **3.2.3:** Pack and prepare any travel-day essentials.
